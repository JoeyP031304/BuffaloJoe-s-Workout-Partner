Privacy & cookie policy

Ironlog — a free personal fitness tracker. https://joeyp031304.github.io/BuffaloJoe-s-Workout-Partner/

Effective October 16, 2026. Applies to the Ironlog web app at https://joeyp031304.github.io/BuffaloJoe-s-Workout-Partner/ and to the copy you add to your Home Screen.

The short version

Ironlog has no accounts and no server of its own. Everything you type or import stays in your browser on your device. We (the person who publishes Ironlog) never receive it, cannot see it, and do not sell or share it. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

Who publishes Ironlog

Ironlog is a free, non-commercial personal project published by Joseph Pracitto (GitHub user JoeyP031304), Florida, United States. Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/JoeyP031304/BuffaloJoe-s-Workout-Partner/issues.

What the app stores on your device

Depending on what you use, Ironlog saves the following in your browser's local storage (the "ironlog.v1" entry): your profile (biological sex, date of birth or age, height, weight), workouts and sets, routines and plans, body measurements, your food log (including any meal photos you attach, kept as small thumbnails), custom foods, daily step counts you import from Apple Health or Samsung Health, workouts you import from Samsung Health, and your settings. Date of birth is used only to decide whether the health-app connection is offered (it is limited to adults) and to unlock it on your 18th birthday.

This data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself (More → Your data & privacy → Export backup). Local storage is tied to one browser: the Safari copy and the Home Screen copy keep separate data, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

What leaves your device, and to whom

  • GitHub Pages (hosting). The app's files are delivered by GitHub. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical request data such as your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or view these logs.
  • Open Food Facts (optional). Only when you scan or type a barcode, or search packaged foods online, Ironlog sends that barcode or search text to the Open Food Facts database (a non-profit) to fetch nutrition facts. Nothing about you is sent with it. See Open Food Facts' privacy page.
  • Apple Shortcuts / iCloud (optional, iPhone). "Get the shortcut" opens a shortcut shared through Apple's iCloud link service. The shortcut runs on your phone, reads your daily step totals from the Health app, and copies them to your clipboard; Ironlog imports them when you tap Paste. Health data is handled on your phone under Apple's rules; it is not sent to us.
  • Samsung Health (optional, Android). You download your own export from Samsung and open it in Ironlog. The file is read on your device and is not uploaded anywhere.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a barcode or attach a meal photo, after your browser asks for permission. Photos are reduced to small thumbnails and stored on the device only.

Cookies and similar technologies

Ironlog sets no cookies. It uses local storage solely to keep your own data and settings on your device (strictly necessary for the app to work), which is why there is no cookie consent request. No third-party cookies, pixels, analytics or advertising technologies are embedded.

Children

Ironlog is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — it is not possible, because nothing is transmitted to us. The health-app connection is only offered to users who are 18 or older.

Your rights and choices

Because all data is on your device and under your control, you can exercise every right yourself at any time: access and portability — More → Export backup (JSON) or the CSV exports; correction — edit anything in the app; deletion — More → Your data & privacy → Delete all my data, or clear this site's data in your browser. We hold no copy, so there is nothing further to delete on our side. If you believe we do hold something about you, or you have any question, open an issue at the address above and we will respond.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Data on your device is protected by your device's own security (passcode, encryption). Anyone with access to your unlocked device and browser could see your Ironlog data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app.
